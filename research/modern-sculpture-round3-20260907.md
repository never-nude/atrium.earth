All accepted 3D files have current CC BY 4.0 source records. Artist credits and underlying artwork rights are recorded separately. Source models came from the public Objaverse archive on Hugging Face, matched by Sketchfab UID and verified by SHA-256.

The Oteiza archive has no textures and uses a neutral material. The Laurent model is the central Venus figure, not the whole fountain. The Firebird is one element of the Stravinsky Fountain. The early Les Baigneuses scan has faceted geometry. Sitting Tattoo X has a soft surface capture and a small gap at the top of the head; these capture limitations have not been reconstructed.

The available Tumbleweed, Tower (DC), Angel of the North and Spider scans were rejected after rendering because of substantial missing or obstructed geometry. Sun God is a modeled reconstruction with an unfinished reverse. No usable open full-sculpture Serra scan was found. Other exclusions and source links are retained in the JSON manifest.

Validation: all eight object pages load the production WebGL viewer; thumbnails and posters match reviewed orientations. Desktop/mobile gallery checks, wing fixtures, asset verification and the production build pass. The preview route is development-only.

The acquisition manifest records source URLs, licenses, collection references, hashes, transformations and preparation notes. Source GLBs, original metadata, reference images and visual review sheets are retained in this worktree’s ignored `.tmp` directory. Optimized GLBs are hosted at the content-addressed URLs recorded in the manifest. The publication checks exercise these remote models through the production viewer.
