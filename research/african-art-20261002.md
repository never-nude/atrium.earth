## Method

- Discovery: Sketchfab search and full account enumeration for museum publishers, filtered to downloadable CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA models with an African provenance signal; museum APIs (Cleveland, Mia, Małopolska) used to confirm departments and metadata.
- Acquisition: licensed archive copies from Zenodo (3D Big Data Space community) or Objaverse 1.0; the Sketchfab download API itself requires a token and was not used.
- Review: duplicate screening against the catalog (Sketchfab UID, Zenodo record, accession, record URL, title); metadata parsed from museum records and scan labels; out-of-scope items (ancient Egypt, non-African objects, undocumented private uploads) excluded and listed in the ingest report.
- Assets: Draco-decoded sources, previews optimized with glTF Transform, thumbnails rendered locally, previews uploaded to R2 under content-hashed names.
