## Capture and metadata notes

- The 26 m Guruslau monument is complete, including all three blades and the relief-covered base; the source thumbnail shows only the lower part.
- The Regina archive contains no surface colour. It uses an explicitly neutral material.
- Le Cheval retains its original vertex colours. An identity white map preserves them through the existing viewer. Date, material and approximate height follow the source contributor; a separate institutional inventory was not found.
- The Trabant sculpture is dated 2005 in its exhibition catalogue, not 1963 as the model title might suggest. Its legacy material was converted to preserve the original diffuse texture.
- Horse Outside is one of two life-size community sculptures, created in 2011 and 2012. The source does not identify which year applies to this horse.
- Meteor is a 182 cm gallery sculpture, included as a life-size contemporary work.
- La Mariposa has minor roughness at the butterfly edges. Consigne a vie retains the darker captured texture on its reverse.
