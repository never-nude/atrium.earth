## Sources and presentation

The selection prioritizes artists not already represented in Atrium. It includes public art, a studio relief, a museum ceramic and two gallery-linked pop-art representations. No prominence ranking is presented to visitors.

- **Ball Games:** Artist, subject, decade and location come from the primary scan contributor. Do not confuse this attribution with the younger sculptor Václav Kyselka, born in 1975. The English title follows the source model title.
- **Ceramic Mosaic Relief:** Primary contributor gives artist, 1983 date, ceramic medium and school. Title is descriptive. Converted the legacy specular-glossiness material to current glTF materials while retaining the source colour texture.
- **Ježek (Hedgehog):** Primary contributor names Karel Kučera and identifies the park and freshly repainted state. Drobné památky independently records the artist and concrete material. No creation date is inferred from the scan title.
- **Dvojice (The Couple):** Primary scan identifies artist, title and 1980s. The regional museum documents Bílek’s public sculpture practice and a marble Dvojice for Hotel Vladimir in 1987, but the scan labels its location City council. Kept the contributor’s decade and city; did not transplant hotel-specific date, material or dimensions without establishing the exact match.
- **To the Letters:** Source model gives G. Malakchiev, title, 1982 and location. Bulgaria’s National Gallery identifies the artist in full as Galin Malakchiev and documents this monumental work at the same site. Exact material and physical dimensions are not established.
- **Torso and Head:** Publisher explicitly calls this his own sculpture and gives plaster medium and approximate scale. The profile links to Tim K Jones’s official Australian artist site. Torso and Head is a concise descriptive title based on the source name.
- **Veuve Clicquot Jerrycan:** Model credits Erik Salin and links the original gallery work. George Thornton Art’s Artsy listing supplies 2020, mixed media and 20 × 20 inches. The artist’s site corroborates the Jerrycan series. This low-polygon model is a representation, not claimed to be a detailed photogrammetric scan.
- **Love Balloon:** Primary model links the original gallery listing. George Thornton Art’s Artsy record gives artist, 2021 and 52 × 15 inches; its archived dealer listing on 1stDibs explicitly describes the red balloon and red base. The current gallery page depicts a green variant. Retained the source-linked gallery’s sole-artist credit rather than applying joint credits from listings of different later variants.
- **Subling:** Museum’s primary model explicitly supplies title, artist, ceramic medium, 1990 date, edition of 50 and collection. Digitised through Scanning the Horizon. Model file is CC0, distinct from the CC BY files elsewhere in this batch.

Two creation dates are unrecorded. Scan and upload dates are kept separate. Missing materials and dimensions are left empty rather than inferred. The school mosaic is explicitly an architectural front-surface capture; the reverse wall was not recorded. The two Paspro360 pieces are low-polygon representations, not described as detailed photogrammetric scans. Model licenses and artwork rights are recorded separately.
