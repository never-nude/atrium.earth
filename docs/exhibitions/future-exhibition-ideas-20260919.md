# Atrium exhibition ideas

Saved 19 September 2026. These ten proposals preserve the approved brainstorm and its linked shortlists for future curation. [The Price of Victory (#2)](https://atrium.earth/exhibitions/the-price-of-victory/) is published; the other nine remain future exhibitions. Repeated works are intentional across these alternative proposals.

Future curation preferences: diversify geography and historical periods; include plenty of contemporary work when it fits; favor artists associated with Dia Beacon where relevant, without requiring the works to come from Dia. Use no more than one Sol LeWitt work in this exhibition spanning millennia.

## 1. Dressing the Self

**Status:** Future exhibition.

How hair, crowns, uniforms, and avatars turn appearance into identity, status, and belonging.

- [Female Head with Braided Hair](https://atrium.earth/works/ancient-near-east/female-head-isin-larsa/) — Mesopotamia, c. 2000–1750 BCE.
- [Head of Amenhotep III Wearing the Blue Crown](https://atrium.earth/works/egyptian/head-of-amenhotep-iii-cleveland/) — Egypt, c. 1391–1353 BCE.
- [Grotesque Helmet](https://atrium.earth/works/renaissance/grotesque-helmet-goldenes-dachl/) — 1515–1525.
- [Lt. Uhura’s Starfleet Uniform](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/uhura-red-starfleet-uniform-nichelle-nichols-nmaahc/) — 1966–1967.
- [Junkanoo Headdress](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/headdress-junkanoo-smithsonian-folklife/) — Bahamas, 1994.
- [Africhibi: Imasunzu](https://atrium.earth/works/africa/africhibi-imasunzu/) — Nairobi Design Week, 2022; drawing on Rwandan hairstyles.

## 2. The Price of Victory

**Status:** Published 19 September 2026 — [open exhibition](https://atrium.earth/exhibitions/the-price-of-victory/).

How art turns war into glory, and how other works bring the wounded, defeated, and bereaved into view.

- [Lying Wounded Warrior](https://atrium.earth/works/greek/lying-wounded-warrior-aphaia-smk-cast/) — Temple of Aphaia, c. 500–490 BCE.
- [Roman Soldiers Attacking](https://atrium.earth/works/roman/trajans-column-roman-soldiers-attacking-smk-cast/) — Trajan’s Column, 113 CE.
- [Vessel with Battle Scene](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/vessel-battle-scene-cleveland/) — Maya, 600–900.
- [Bayon Naval Battle Relief](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/bayon-naval-battle-relief-cast-guimet-threedscans/) — Cambodia, 12th–13th-century relief; cast 1904.
- [The Dying Tecumseh](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/the-dying-tecumseh-smithsonian/) — Ferdinand Pettrich, carved 1856.
- [Mourning Victory](https://atrium.earth/works/mourning-victory-melvin-memorial/) — Daniel Chester French, 1906–1915.
- [Stop War](https://atrium.earth/works/europe/frank-fenriz-stop-war/) — Frank Fenriz, 2022.

## 3. Small Worlds

**Status:** Future exhibition.

Why we shrink buildings, landscapes, and machines into objects we can grasp—and what that reduction lets us understand or control.

- [Terracotta Model of a Warship](https://atrium.earth/works/greek/terracotta-warship-cyprus-fitzwilliam/) — Cyprus, 300–201 BCE.
- [Nayarit House Model](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/house-model-nayarit/) — Mexico, 200 BCE–300 CE.
- [Bottle with Mountain Sacrifice Scene](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/stirrup-spout-bottle-mountain-sacrifice-met/) — Moche, Peru, 500–800.
- [Table Fountain](https://atrium.earth/works/medieval/table-fountain-cleveland/) — c. 1320–1340.
- [Thomas Elkins’s Refrigerator Patent Model](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/refrigerator-patent-model-thomas-elkins-nmaahc/) — 1879.
- [Subling](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/mckendrick-subling/) — Tom McKendrick, 1990.
- [Sténos, Maquette](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/posth-kohler-stenos-maquette/) — David Posth-Kohler, 2019; a preparatory sculpture for a monumental installation.

## 4. Ideal Bodies—For Whom?

How sculptors choose which bodies embody beauty, strength, age, or difference—and how those choices change.

- [Doryphoros](https://atrium.earth/works/doryphoros/) — c. 450 BCE.
- [Old Fisherman](https://atrium.earth/works/old-fisherman/) — 3rd century BCE.
- [Sleeping Hermaphroditus](https://atrium.earth/works/roman/sleeping-hermaphroditus-louvre-ma-231/) — 100–150 CE; Bernini’s mattress, 1620.
- [Les Baigneuses](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/saint-phalle-les-baigneuses/) — Niki de Saint Phalle, 1985.

- [Two Reclining Figures on a Calder Base](https://atrium.earth/works/europe/rogiers-two-reclining-figures/) — Peter Rogiers, 2006–2018.

- [Helen Adams Keller](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/helen-adams-keller-ruotolo-smithsonian/) — Onorio Ruotolo, 1916.

- [Homenaje a la Mujer Pastusa](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/homenaje-mujer-pastusa-narino/) — Colombia, 2019.

- [Embracing Couple](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/embracing-couple-thailand-mia/) — Thailand, 1st century BCE–1st century CE.
- [Somaskanda, Holy Family](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/somaskanda-holy-family-mia/) — India, 13th–14th century.
- [Saint Martin and the Beggar](https://atrium.earth/works/medieval/saint-martin-beggar-serra-mia/) — c. 1375–1385.
- [Death and the Mother](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/death-and-the-mother-hansen-jacobsen-smk/) — Niels Hansen Jacobsen, 1892.
- [Untitled, Woman and Child](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/untitled-woman-and-child-selma-burke-smithsonian/) — Selma Burke, c. 1950.
- [Stop War](https://atrium.earth/works/europe/frank-fenriz-stop-war/) — Frank Fenriz, 2022; a child protecting her teddy bear.

## 7. Art Made by Rules

**Status:** Future exhibition.

How repetition, symmetry, and prescribed operations generate complex forms—from patterned vessels to conceptual and digital sculpture.

- [Jar with Impressed Decoration](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/impressed-design-jar-thailand-mia/) — Thailand, c. 1000 BCE.
- [Hu Wine Vessel with Geometric Decoration](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/hu-geometric-inlay-mia/) — China, 5th–4th century BCE.
- [Diaguita Bowl with Zigzag Decoration](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/diaguita-zigzag-bowl-mnhn/) — Chile, 1000–1400.
- [Mamluk Door Panel](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/mamluk-door-panel-mia/) — 14th century.
- [Cubic Construction: Diagonal 4](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/lewitt-cubic-construction-diagonal-four/) — Sol LeWitt, 1971; digital reconstruction.
- [Gurunsi Plank Mask](https://atrium.earth/works/africa/gurunsi-plank-mask/) — West Africa, c. 1990.
- [Data-Confluences 006: Africa](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/ribeiro-data-confluences-006/) — Clarissa Ribeiro, 2021; environmental recordings translated into digital form.

Where function, beauty, ritual, and status meet—and what happens when a useful container becomes a sculpture.

- [Female Statue-Vessel from Kaluraz](https://atrium.earth/works/ancient-near-east/kaluraz-female-statue-vessel-louvre-ao-32465/) — Iran, 1000–700 BCE.
- [Moche Anthropomorphic Vessel](https://atrium.earth/works/americas/moche-anthropomorphic-vessel-mnhn/) — Peru, 100–700 CE.
- [Horse Aquamanile](https://atrium.earth/works/medieval/horse-aquamanile-hunt/) — 15th century; a sculptural hand-washing vessel.
- [Woman-shaped Bottle](https://atrium.earth/works/europe/woman-shaped-bottle-stockholm-1764/) — probably 1764.

## 10. The Stories We Keep Retelling

**Status:** Future exhibition.

What changes when artists reinvent Hercules, Shiva, and Eve for a different age? This would unfold through three sets of comparisons.

- [Head from the Farnese Hercules Type](https://atrium.earth/works/head-from-farnese-hercules-type/) — 4th century BCE.
- [Ptolemaic Ruler in the Guise of Hercules](https://atrium.earth/works/egyptian/ptolemaic-ruler-hercules-mia/) — 2nd century BCE.
- [Shiva Nataraja](https://atrium.earth/works/asia/shiva-nataraja-after-conservation-mia/) — c. 1100.
- [Eve, Reduced Version](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/eve-rodin-reduced-marble/) — Auguste Rodin, 1892.
- [Hercules at Sancti Petri](https://atrium.earth/works/contemporary/hercules-sancti-petri/) — Mario César de las Cuevas, 2010.
- [Shiva Nataraja](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/pradakaran-govindan-pratiban-shiva-nataraja/) — Sri Pradakaran, Sri Govindan, and Sri Pratiban, 2013.
- [Eve, or Temptation](https://atrium.earth/works/modern/eve-temptation-belin/) — Yannick Belin, exhibited 2022.
