# Collection surface review — 6 November 2026

## Scope and outcome

Screened all 3,019 public-work thumbnails in the 3,037-record catalog snapshot, including a separate review of 84 effective exposure settings at or above 0.9. This was collection-wide thumbnail screening followed by targeted source-image and live-viewer comparisons; it was not a full interactive inspection of every model. Publisher thumbnails are appearance references, not calibrated physical-color measurements.

Twenty-two works receive individually reviewed exposure corrections. The rock-crystal chess bishop additionally receives a compatible material derivative. No global lighting, shadows, viewer controls, geometry, orientations, exhibition membership, or collection visibility is changed.

## Crystal material compatibility

The previous viewing GLB depended on `KHR_materials_pbrSpecularGlossiness`, leaving its diffuse map and transparency unavailable to the current loader. A derivative of that existing viewing GLB converts the material workflow with glTF Transform 4.4.2 `metalRough`. The original local preview and canonical source reference remain intact. This is a repair of the existing viewing derivative, not a new full-resolution source scan.

- New derivative: `preview-c773251af203.glb`, 4,738,216 bytes; SHA-256 `c773251af2031bcf9c753601913e4403e52cf5cf1b8a5d4394cda6fcea9fbb5c`.
- Decoded geometry, UVs, normals, and node transforms match before conversion, after conversion, and after re-reading; geometry fingerprint `254f7ad2bbb680d0f2c950cc78852db61e82581f7c7dc1a5a573944938327ef2`.
- Original diffuse/emissive/normal image bytes, diffuse and alpha factors, alpha modes, and normal scale are retained. Conversion rewrites material-workflow texture channels; no new simplification, surface painting, or texture compression.
- Source/current/converted comparison and four actual orbit views were inspected. Identical highlights across different rendering environments are not claimed.

## Source limitations and deferred work

O’Carolan’s Harp contains no photographic textures: its six primitives have uniform gray vertex color. Its label now makes that limitation explicit; exposure remains unchanged. No wood color is invented.

Warrior with Shield, Orlando Furioso, and Durham Still Life have documented colorless viewing models, so exposure is not a remedy for absent surface capture. Nude in the Sun remains a surface-fidelity investigation; Mattei Amazon remains a possible shading review. No changes are made to these deferred cases. Naturally pale plaster/stone and other source-matched works remain unchanged.
