# Seven additional public-download sculptures

Seven new works continue `sensual-sculpture-20260919`, bringing the published batch to 19. Twelve original shortlist compositions are represented, with seven thematic additions or substitutes. Forty-three exact original selections remain unpublished. See [counting and source audit](final-pass-acquisition-audit.md).

All seven downloads used public access. Login-dependent sources were skipped at the user’s request. Scan licenses are CC BY 4.0 and each work credits its creator and source.

## Review and limits

The three Rodin scans preserve their full source triangle counts and retained display bases. They have limited surface detail and no captured texture; bronze appearance is an explicit display treatment. Exact loaned-cast records distinguish the Cantor Foundation from the Iris Cantor collection and the Portland scan venue.

Naiad and Île-de-France retain all source faces, with compressed geometry and resized textures. The two South Asian scans were simplified to approximately 250,000 triangles. Yakshi is upright and shows the complete surviving fragment; small rear gaps remain. Its date and origin are undocumented and omitted. Amaravati is a scan of the carved front, with flat closure surfaces and incomplete sides/reverse. These limits appear in public work notes.

Diana was held after visual review found a missing bow and ragged pedestal geometry. A mislabeled Three Graces scan was rejected as a different composition already represented in the catalog. Login requirements, restrictive scan licenses, uncertain work rights and absent suitable models remain documented source constraints, not a claim that every possible source has been exhausted.

All seven have reviewed orientations, real thumbnails, source/license attribution, and explicit physical-dimension review entries. No strict physical scale calibration is claimed. The companion acquisition audit was prepared before final geometry QA; all seven proposed works subsequently passed the qualified catalog-display review documented in the publication manifest.

## Validation

Wing assignment, newest-additions grouping, catalog assets and production build passed. Build produced 3,193 static pages. All 1,217 earlier catalog records, their timestamps and existing runtime metadata are preserved. The catalog now contains 1,224 records, of which 1,212 are public.

Whole-catalog physical-dimension and spatial-eligibility checks have known pre-existing missing-review failures outside this batch; these unrelated records were not modified.
