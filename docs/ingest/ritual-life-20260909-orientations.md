# Orientation review — Daily life and ceremony

Every model was inspected from six directions. These are the selected default views.

| Work | Visual check |
| --- | --- |
| Yong bell | Three-quarter view shows the bosses, relief ornament and long suspension shank. Original pale untextured area at the lower opening remains visible. |
| Gothic Chalice | Cup, jeweled ornament, stem figures and six-lobed foot are upright and fully framed. |
| Vessel for Washing Feet (Lavabo ad Mandatum) | Elevated view reveals the lobed basin interior, lion-mouth ring handle and supporting feet. |
| Ewer with bird, vegetal, and calligraphic motifs | Spout and handle are both visible, with the base level and entire tall blue vessel framed; original pale areas at the foot remain. |
| Ewer with cover | View retains the seated cover, short spout, handle and rounded celadon body. |
| Bowl with abstract (Anthropomorphic?) design | Elevated camera reveals the central blue design and the full interior, while the bowl remains level. |
| Pair of Rattles | Elevated view presents both decorated gourd heads with their handles pointing downward; the cataloged pair stays together. |
| Xu Food Vessel with Lid, One of a Pair | Decorated face, paired handles and seated lid face the viewer. Reverse inspected: the source has incomplete texture coverage there, which is preserved. |
| Cloisonné Incense Burner | Covered burner stands level on its three feet, with its finial and enamel decoration fully framed. |
| Fang Reliquary Offering Box | Open compartments and carved side masks face the viewer; the seated figure and complete box remain upright, with the plain back inspected. |
| Incense Burner with Female Deity | Turned to the modeled face and headdress; the footed vessel remains upright, with original cracks and rear losses preserved. |
| Epitaph tablet of Prince Yuan Mi | Inscribed face opens toward the viewer with all edges retained. This is a surface scan; the same text appears mirrored from behind rather than presenting a separately captured reverse. |
| Drum from Yunnan | Elevated camera shows the drawn figures on the drumhead and its shallow rim; the drum remains horizontal. |
| Whistling Effigy Jar | View shows the musician, connected chambers, bridge and spout together; the complete ceramic vessel is retained. |
| Door Panel | Carved geometric face opens toward the viewer with the full frame retained. This is a surface scan; the back presents the same surface in reverse. |
| Calligraphy brush | Carved handle stands above the surviving bristles; the complete length is framed. |
| Diaguita Stone Wind Instrument | Elevated three-quarter view reveals the four upper tubes, long stone body and original inventory markings. |
| Stone Wind Instrument from Collipulli | Elevated three-quarter view reveals the stone instrument’s upper sound chambers and narrow profile; catalog markings remain visible. |
| Pyx (Ciborium) | Covered cup, finial, ornamented stem and complete foot stay upright and within the frame. |
| Woman-shaped Bottle | Front view shows the skirt, surviving torso and arm handle. The missing head-stopper and broken upper edges are part of the historical fragment. |
| Snuff Bottle with European Figures | Decorated broad face opens toward the viewer; both painted sides inspected. This model represents the bottle body only. |
| Carved Ritual Tray | Carved figure stands above the tray, facing forward; the complete tray and plain reverse were inspected. |
| Two-handled Vessel | Two handles flank the neck symmetrically, with the full rounded ceramic body and base retained. |
| Figural Lamp Stand | Bacchus faces the viewer above the complete stepped lamp-stand base and animal-paw feet; reverse inspected. |
| Box for Effervescent Bath Tablets | Elevated view rotates the printed Velosa label and bathing illustration upright; all four corners and the box edges are retained. |
| He wine vessel | Three-quarter view separates the projecting spout from the curved handle and retains all three legs. |
| Bianhu wine vessel | Broad face shows the copper-inlaid rectangular panels; flattened profile and opposite face inspected. |
| Bowl Depicting a Lady Riding an Elephant | Elevated view turns the central elephant and rider upright. Framing tightened for this source scene while retaining the complete rim and painted interior. |
| Bowl Depicting a Seated King with Attendants | Elevated view turns the seated king and attendants upright, with the entire painted bowl and rim retained. |
| Sultanabad Ware Bowl | Elevated camera reveals the birds and floral decoration inside the level bowl; complete rim retained. |
