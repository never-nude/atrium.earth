# Featured collection — 15 October 2026

Exactly 250 public works are approved for editorial features. This is a curatorial selection, not a numerical ranking of the whole collection. The first eight are the museum overview highlights. The homepage draws from the works marked “Hero,” subject to the 15 MiB loading limit. Newest Additions continues to show actual imports independently.

Current thumbnail, identity, surface presentation and collection breadth informed the choices. A cast can qualify on its own terms; undocumented reconstructions, conspicuous scan failures and unclear reuse grants were excluded. This review does not claim a new full-model geometry or primary-license audit of every legacy record.
