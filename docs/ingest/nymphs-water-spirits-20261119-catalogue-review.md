# Nymph and water-spirit catalogue additions

Owner scope: catalogue pieces only. No exhibition, exhibition index or exhibition filters are added. Baseline is deployed main `d0bb92fb`.

## Catalogue match and accepted additions

All twelve supplied existing slugs were found. The other eighteen exact physical objects were absent after a full source UID, institution, accession and version audit. Fifteen new records are accepted: six unchanged licensed GLBs and nine credited photographs. Three remain held. The public catalogue grows from 3,184 to 3,199 works.

The regular Collection provides search, date ordering, maker, period and material filters; all accepted entries have canonical artwork pages. Every one of the twenty-seven included records has an original researched paragraph with public source links. Rights apply to the specific capture, and the site MIT license does not cover collection assets.

## Identity and rights decisions

- Bernini is the Borghese original, using Daderot’s CC0 photograph. The supplied scan has insufficient original-versus-cast evidence and is not republished as that original. Soldani’s Cleveland terracotta remains a separate record.
- Cellini is the Louvre bronze MR 1706. The licensed photograph’s Escalier Mollien setting matches the Louvre record and the museum’s [2024 brochure](https://api-www.louvre.fr/sites/default/files/2024-05/Cercle%20Louvre%20Entreprises%20-%20Brochure.pdf), pages 2 and 8; it is not treated as Barye’s later Caryatids-room cast.
- Godecharle is Louvre terracotta RF 4666, 1787, not the 1804 Brussels stone group. Bartolini is Louvre marble RF 4451; Coulon is Saintes 1908.1.65B; Injalbert is Orsay’s 1905 marble RF 1402.
- The Met’s current Nymph and Satyr record identifies marble and Italian/Tuscan origin. Its historical Zacchi attribution stays qualified. The Huntington bronze remains nineteenth-century French, formerly attributed to Clodion.
- Mirbach is the 1981 physical bronze cast after Tilgner’s 1875–77 model. The chronological record uses the cast date and does not mistake the earlier model date for this bronze’s manufacture.
- The current source for Sellier’s reused scan names Toulouse, while the Bayssan commission page names a 2021 sculpture of the same title, maker and marble. Its slug is retained, the unverified location/title qualifier is removed, and the disagreement is stated on the artwork page.
- Current primary records supply production evidence for Cellini in Paris, Echo in Rome and the Coulon vessel at Sèvres. Sestino has a documented Roman findspot; Haerdtl has its original Vienna fountain setting. Museum holding locations are not used as manufacturing evidence for the other additions.
- Six individually reviewed noncommercial scan grants retain their exact creators and terms. All six provider GLBs remain byte-identical; the Coulon NoDerivatives file receives no geometry/material conversion. Other models are not made to look like this batch.
- Photographs retain background and framing; display copies are resized without cropping. Credits and CC/PD or institutional educational/noncommercial terms remain visible in Details & sources. The Huntington photo is not called CC0.
- Existing Calder, Schwaiger and Sellier captures remain reused from the baseline, with their source licenses preserved. This does not establish all underlying artwork rights. PAFA explicitly lists an estate/ARS notice for Calder; those existing modern-work questions require separate rights review. No blanket all-adult assertion is made about the list or the small supporting figures in Soldani’s group.

## Release result

Review `44661225754` was cancelled by this release coordinator during its build after a date audit found four new labels that the existing conservative globe parser could not read. Both live CDN gates were skipped; no live sweep began. The corrections separate the physical creation date from attribution/prototype wording: Mirbach `1981 cast`, Bartolini `c. 1835–1845`, Clytie `1872 carved`, and the NGA carving `Mid-late 18th century`. Maker/history retain the other information. Four actual-record regression assertions verify the physical spans; the date parser and its legitimate uncertainty checks are unchanged.

The full dedicated-runner [review 44662178126](https://github.com/never-nude/atrium.earth/actions/runs/44662178126) passed on `0691d7fb`: 3,204 model URLs, six first-view hashes and 3,119 CDN JPEG URLs passed on the first attempt. All eighty-four browser cases, four globe profiles, gestures, performance, Collection and published-image checks passed. The review package contains 25,085 files / 908,957,726 bytes.

A final offline-tool compatibility fix adds explicit image bindings to storage dependency discovery. It validates local image path, stable bytes and full hash, rejects model/image conflicts, and protects image files and aliases under existing local-preview rules. Older worktrees may omit the image map. Remote GLB receipt/CORS/deletion rules are unchanged. The full storage suite passed forty-six Node tests, including sixty safety acceptance/rejection checks in its dependency test; the full default Node suite also passed again with 140 experience tests and no skips. The asset verifier now rejects null model/image conflicts consistently.

Those final changes affect only offline validators/tests and documentation. The site source, assets, package configuration and workflows are byte-identical to the passed review head. The normal production workflow will recheck the final merged head before publication. No production deployment has begun yet.
