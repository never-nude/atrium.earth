# Thirty more works across time and place — 9 September 2026

Adds 30 distinct museum objects to production commit `4dfe44b9c9b30b76c0c01a836673ad7612dcc259`, taking the public collection from 630 to 660 works (668 catalog records, including eight hidden records). Existing records and their viewing settings are preserved.

The selection spans approximately 2000 BCE to the first half of the twentieth century, with undated objects retained as undated. It broadens coverage across Asia, Africa, Europe, North and South America, and Oceania. Greenland, Kiribati, Papua New Guinea, Indonesia, Myanmar, Afghanistan, and Ecuador are among the places represented.

## Source checks

Every model has a fresh museum-account Sketchfab metadata response and a licensed Zenodo archive record. Object URLs, accessions, and scan IDs were checked against the existing catalog; alternate scans of the same object were excluded. Current museum records supply titles, materials, dates, and places where available. Creation dates are not inferred from accession, discovery, or digitization dates.

The Thai water-buffalo vessel is dated 1000–300 BCE in the current Mia record. Somaskanda is dated to the thirteenth–fourteenth century. The Korean scroll box is dated to the nineteenth century in the current Cleveland record. The Greenland carving has a museum production range of 1480–1550 and a recorded radiocarbon result of 1480. The Moche and Pitrén ranges are expressly labeled as cultural periods. The Chilean chachapuma is described as Tiwanaku-style, with Chile recorded as its findspot.

The Kiribati helmet’s accession history remains under investigation by its museum. The Punu mask’s specific attribution to Equatorial Guinea comes from the holding institution; its general geography is recorded as Central Africa. Undated pieces retain blank creation dates. Thorvaldsen metadata is supported by the previously cached official museum record and a fresh museum scan record; the museum API returned 503 during this release.

A Fang offering-box archive was rejected after visual comparison with the museum’s current model. The published batch uses the Punu mask instead.

## Assets and verification

The 30 original GLBs were checked against the archive checksums, validated as self-contained glTF 2 files, and optimized for browser delivery. Final previews total 28.88 MB. Mesh transforms and source textures are retained. Actual triangle counts are taken from each GLB rather than potentially different web-viewer statistics.

All 30 model previews use immutable content-hashed URLs on the existing Atrium R2 domain. The accompanying JSON manifest records primary source URLs, licenses, source and preview hashes, triangle counts, viewing settings, thumbnail hashes, and public asset verification results.

All 30 starting views and thumbnails were visually reviewed. The walking Buddha was turned to the front, the falcon was presented in profile, and the coconut cup was rotated to show its opening and exterior. The long scroll box and Greenland carving remain horizontal. The shell gorget’s front was confirmed against the museum’s interactive model; its exposure was reduced to make the carving easier to read.

Validation: wing classification, catalog asset verification, production build, preserved baseline records, unique new objects, compiled orientation settings, matching thumbnails, and remote model hashes. The development-only review route is `/preview/across-time-and-place-round2/`; it is excluded from the production build.
