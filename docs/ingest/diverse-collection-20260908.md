Add 30 museum-sourced works to Atrium, increasing the public collection from 600 to 630. The selection spans approximately 5500 BCE to the nineteenth century and broadens representation across Africa, Asia, the Americas, Oceania, Europe, Egypt, and western Asia.

Highlights include a prehistoric clay figure from Modlnica, a gold pendant from Panama, Maya and Zapotec ceramics, three Rapa Nui wood figures, West African masks, a Gandharan Buddha, Sangay Donyotrubpa (Amoghasiddhi) from Tibet, a two-sided Chinese Buddhist stele, Japanese netsuke, and a Hellenistic terracotta warship from Cyprus.

Every new work includes an institutional source link, current scan license, source and preview hashes, a compressed 3D model on Atrium’s existing R2 domain, and a rendered WebP thumbnail. Seventeen scans use CC0 and thirteen use CC BY 4.0. Nine works retain an unknown creation date because their source records do not establish one.

The existing 608 catalog records remain unchanged. A development-only review page at `/preview/across-time-and-place/` shows the new batch together.

Validation: production build passed; wing acceptance fixtures passed (630 public works, zero unfiled); all 638 catalog records passed asset-configuration checks; all 30 new public model URLs returned HTTP 200 with expected content type, CORS, file length, and SHA-256. All 30 thumbnails were visually reviewed. Preview downloads total 42.56 MB, compared with 1,018.29 MB of archived originals.
