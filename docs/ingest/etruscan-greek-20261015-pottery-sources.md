## Scope qualifications

- Whole conserved museum pots may contain physical restoration. This is recorded separately from digital reconstruction.
- Source-thumbnail screening is a preliminary check; complete surfaces, cavity/handle geometry, orientation and material fidelity require the actual downloaded source and generated views.
- Every unchanged provider source, texture/material dependency and provenance/license record must enter the permanent private verified Drive archive, with a verified recovery map, before any eligible local offload. Temporary Actions source artifacts and optimized R2 previews are distinct.
- No component selection exemptions, invented crop regions, weak geometry thresholds or exact-scale claims are proposed.
- The v3 detail endpoint encountered429 responses after a shared burst. Subsequent public website model metadata is checked at bounded rate, with no credentials or payload downloads.
