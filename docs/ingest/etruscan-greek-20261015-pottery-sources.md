# Ancient Greek pottery discovery — 2026-10-15

40 recommended museum/university-source vessels, 10 dated reserves, six reserves held for chronology/culture evidence and one source-scope hold. No model payload has been acquired. Selected GLB bytes and measured archive geometry remain pending authenticated preflight and source inspection.

## Selection and source qualifications

- Recommended replacements from the initial contact sheets: Met 74.51.965, the Aristonothos krater and Castello Banfi 261 replace Bryn Mawr P.1490 and the two more damaged Fralin vessels. P.1490 is held; Fralin 1983.2.5 and 1973.71 are qualified dated reserves.
- Canonical v3 model URLs identify each license record; the actual observed public metadata endpoint, license object, date and download/geometry fields are retained in `raw_api_evidence`. Only CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA models are eligible. Original upload archive sizes are not represented as selected GLB sizes.
- All 57 source UIDs are new against the 1951-record catalog. All 56 main/reserve source-record URLs and institution/accession pairs are distinct. Known same-object alternate scans and noncommercial/no-derivatives models were excluded.
- Physical conservation and reconstruction are part of the scanned object. Cleveland 1915.718 has documented modern physical reconstruction; 1923.645 is reassembled from fragments with losses. These are not anonymous digital reconstructions.
- The Empordà three vessels retain small white museum display mounts. The Fralin pyxis has its lid digitized separately. No mount removal, component merge, invented crop region or geometry-threshold exemption is proposed.
- Hunt HCM 234 is captured with vertex colors and zero image textures. HCM 235 has conflicting appearance metadata (zero textures/no vertex colors, but a colored thumbnail and a source JPEG listing); actual source materials and dependencies must settle that discrepancy before promotion.
- Artwork origin, probable findspot, and holding collection remain distinct. Hadra is Unfiled: the Cretan production tradition and probable Alexandrian findspot do not establish this vessel’s exact manufacture. Euboean Met krater is not assigned to Cyprus from its reported findspot.
- Metric dimensions are published metric values or disclosed conversions at 2.54 cm/in, rounded to 0.1 cm. Unlabelled measurement sequences and source discrepancies do not authorize exact scale or AR. Current Met dimensions 115.6 × 57.2 cm supersede neither silently nor geometrically the uploader’s conflicting 130.8 cm; the conflict remains a calibration blocker.

## Newly resolved primary evidence

- Bonn 79: [CVA Bonn 1, printed pp.14–15 / PDF pp.12–13](https://archiv.badw.de/de/002726979/002726979.pdf) identifies Eton-Nika Painter, 380/370 BCE and H 33.4 cm / rim 35.7 cm / foot 16 cm. This is not Polion inventory 78.
- Hunt HCM 234: [museum lesson plan](https://www.huntmuseum.com/assets/uploads/2022/10/Lesson-Plan-2-Layout.pdf) dates the matching lebes gamikos to the 4th century BCE.
- NCMA primary pages supply exact accessions, Syriskos Painter and Darius Painter workshop attributions, Campanian guttus chronology, and the qualified Hadra history.
- [Met object240553](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/240553) and its [public API record](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/240553) give current 115.6 × 57.2 cm dimensions and Euboean production.
- [Capitoline Aristonothos object record](https://www.museicapitolini.org/en/opera/cratere-con-l%E2%80%99accecamento-di-polifemo-e-battaglia-navale) confirms author, terracotta, subject and second-quarter 7th-century BCE date. The model’s initial “Roman” wording is not adopted. The quoted Caere/Greek-immigrant context remains qualified.
- [University of Virginia 3D Greek Vases](https://archaeology.virginia.edu/3d-greek-vases) identifies actual Fralin museum originals, dated inventories and color laser scanning. Shared project URLs are related evidence, not reused exact object URLs.
