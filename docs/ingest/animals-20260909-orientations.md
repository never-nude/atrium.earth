# Orientation review — Animals, real and imagined

Every model was inspected from six directions. These are the selected default views.

| Work | Visual check |
| --- | --- |
| Sphinx | Three-quarter front view shows the human face, lion body and paws; full architectural plinth stays level. |
| Aquatic Sea Horse and Osprey | Three-quarter front view shows the sea horse, curved neck, osprey and aquatic tail; complete pedestal retained. |
| Reclining Bison | Side view shows the bison’s head, curved horns and reclining body; small patch of scanned ground retained. |
| Horse with Rider Figurine | Side view shows horse and rider together; ancient losses to the rider and legs are preserved. |
| Bird-headed Genie Plaque | Turned to the decorated face so the bird-headed figure is visible; plain reverse inspected. |
| Elephant Attacking a Feline | Rotated the pavement mosaic to face the viewer, with elephant and feline upright; the complete rectangular panel is retained. |
| Gold Lion | Three-quarter view shows the lion’s open mouth, four legs and handle; gold surface and reverse inspected. |
| Wolf with deer leg | Side view shows the wolf’s face, ribs and deer leg; carving is upright and complete. |
| Vessel in the Form of a Fish | Broadside view shows the painted fish, tail and bridge handle; entire vessel retained. |
| Fox Priest | Front view shows the fox’s face, robe and shoulder staff; rear and suspension openings inspected. |
| Guardian Lion | Three-quarter front view shows the lion’s face, chest and haunches; ancient leg losses remain visible. |
| Tiger | Side view reveals the tiger’s full body, curled tail and socket; feet remain level. |
| Dog | Three-quarter view shows the dog’s profile, incised body and open vessel spout; fully upright. |
| You wine vessel in double-owl shape | Frontal view shows the owl faces, four legs and seated lid; uses the assembled scan rather than an exploded presentation. |
| Toad sitting on a straw sandal | Three-quarter side view reveals the toad, woven sandal and strap; full small carving retained. |
| Lime Jar in the Form of an Elephant | Frontal view shows elephant eyes, trunk and lid; rounded body and rear inspected. |
| Aquamanile (Ram-shaped Ewer) | Three-quarter front view shows the ram’s spout, horns, decorated body and handle; all feet framed. |
| Finial with animal | Side view shows the animal’s long legs and cylindrical fitting; turquoise inlay and reverse inspected. |
| Apis Bull | Three-quarter side view makes the bull’s body, horns and sun disk legible; complete display base retained. |
| Kappa and toad hand-wrestling on a leaf | View shows both the kappa and toad facing each other on the leaf; full carving and leaf edge retained. |
| Rat on persimmon | Side view shows the rat on its persimmon, including the tail and carved fruit; whole netsuke retained. |
| Karikpo Antelope Mask | Turned to the carved antelope face, with both long horns upright; hollow rear inspected. |
| Crocodile Plank Mask | Rotated upright with the long tail above the head, matching the documented wearing position; decorated front selected and entire mask retained. |
| Maya Bird Whistle | Three-quarter view shows the bird’s head, beak and carved wing; whistle opening on the reverse inspected. |
| Horse Aquamanile | Side view shows the horse’s raised foreleg, spout and dragon handle holding the reins; full vessel framed. |
| Thoth as a Baboon | Three-quarter front view shows the seated baboon’s head, chest and paws; decorated base remains level. |
| Snake curled around a turtle | Three-quarter view shows the turtle’s head and snake coiled around its shell; all sides inspected. |
| Indra Riding His Royal Elephant (Airavata) | Face-on view presents the complete recorded relief. The source captures the carved surface only; its material is visible from both sides during rotation, without adding a reverse. |
| Crocodile | Elevated three-quarter view shows the crocodile’s long body, head, legs and carved back; kept in its natural horizontal position. |
| Vessel in the Shape of the Polish Eagle | Broadside three-quarter view shows the eagle’s head, spread wing, orb and entire ceremonial base. |
