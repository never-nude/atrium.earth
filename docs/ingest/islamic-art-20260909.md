# Arts of the Islamic world — 50 additions

This selection explores art from Islamic societies, including religious, courtly and domestic objects. The batch adds 50 distinct works to production commit `68cb88df809cacda1fc81c17b6f82248844c2ce5`, increasing the public collection from 1000 to 1050. The catalog also retains eight previously hidden records.

## Selection and sources

Ceramics, calligraphy, metalwork, carved objects, and architectural ornament form this selection. Every work is assigned to an existing wing. Model identifiers, collection links, accession numbers and visual references were checked against the baseline to avoid adding another scan of the same object. Paired or assembled objects are counted once.

## Catalog qualifications

The current Hamburg museum record dates basin 1873.192 to 1800–1805, replacing the older date in its scan description. The tombstone of Ali ibn Saad uses inventory 2021.46 from the source record heading; the same scan description contains a conflicting repeated inventory number in its final line. The source for the Murcia oratory presents its identification as probable. Individual manufacture dates remain unconfirmed for several architectural fragments, furniture objects and the powder horn. The mashrabiya source does not identify a precise location; its African wing placement is a curatorial grouping, not a claimed findspot.

## Model preparation and review

Original files were matched to the published archive sizes and checksums. Direct archive links and the public Objaverse mirror supply identical files; actual retrieval URLs are recorded in the manifest. The 50 optimized previews total 81.32 MB and use immutable content-hashed URLs on Atrium’s model domain.

The model license applies to the downloaded archive version. This does not change rights in separate museum photographs or imply that every asset on a museum website shares the model’s license. Attribution identifies the source contributor and Atrium’s geometry and texture optimization. Unknown manufacture dates and materials remain blank; acquisition dates are not substituted for creation dates.

Each model was reviewed from six directions, with additional adjusted views when needed. Saved camera angles, object rotation and framing also generate its thumbnail. Original breaks, repairs, display supports, untextured surfaces and limits of surface-only scans remain part of the source representation. The orientation report records relevant limitations.

## Verification

Wing fixtures, asset completeness and production build passed. Release validation checks unique additions, unchanged baseline records and viewing settings, compiled orientations, model hashes, thumbnails and posters. Public remote models were downloaded and checked for matching bytes, content type and CORS. Browser review covers the new gallery and interactive object viewing. After deployment, all new live pages, thumbnail checksums, model sizes and starting transforms are checked again.

The local review page is `/preview/islamic-art/`; production builds exclude this preview route.
