# Phone AR scale follow-up — 12 September 2026

Laocoön had documented dimensions but no geometry-matched physical reference, so phone AR used the generic normalized display size. The exact scan depicts the historical extended-arm restoration. It now uses a uniform height of 2.42 m, including its integral altar and sculptural base, without added furniture.

[SMK KAS385](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KAS385) explicitly records 242 cm gross height and Berlin mould 263. [Berlin’s R-00263 master](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/laokoon-gruppe-02ba6929c7154fc3af9943f1b73cb656) independently records 242 cm for the extended-arm version. [The museum’s collection description](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/gipsformerei/collection-areas/antiquity/) confirms original-size Laocoön casts. The [current Vatican group’s 208 × 163 × 112 cm](https://www.digitalsculpture.org/laocoon/index02.html) remains separate evidence for its bent-arm restoration.

The raised historical hand controls both the top and the leftmost extent, so neither the current original’s height nor width can calibrate this model. At 2.42 m high, the retained mesh is approximately 1.801 m wide and 1.069 m deep. Historical sources disagree on width/depth (SMK 162.5/103 cm; Berlin 184/100 cm), so those dimensions are not independently matched or distorted. [Geometry review](laocoon-geometry.json) records the exact asset and observations; [accepted records](review.json) retain sources and measurement scope.

Five further reviewed heights now set uniform scale: Henri IV enfant (60 cm), Child with Dog (66 cm), Two Greyhounds (58 cm), Pan and Bacchus (35 cm surviving fragment), and the SMK Esquiline Venus scan (155 cm, excluding a separate modern museum base). Greyhounds and Pan retain explicit discrepancies in their horizontal extents. This brings the collection to 379 documented calibrations and four approximate starting sizes; 663 entries still lack calibrated scale.

Apple Quick Look now receives `allowsContentScaling=0` for a physical reference, as it already did for a virtual stand. Its generated geometry retains the prepared size; unknown-size models without stands remain adjustable. Apple documents this parameter in its [AR Quick Look session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10604/). This fixes an allowed-resizing policy; it does not establish that a specific phone automatically resized the model.

Validation passed: dimension-record checks, spatial/device tests, serialized Quick Look tests, the 3,726-page production build, and Chrome/WebKit browser regressions. The actual 390 px phone-layout Prepare AR action generated a USDZ with a height of 2.420074 m, within 0.1 mm of 2.42 m; the residual comes from the source model’s normalized 16-bit vertex coordinates. Its transform is uniformly 2.42, no virtual stand is present, the base is at floor level within that tolerance, and the AR link disables content scaling. No browser errors or warnings occurred. [Serialized browser result](local-browser-result.json). The local research directory `work/atrium-phone-scale` retains the actual UI-produced USDZ, screenshot, and reproducible browser check. Native iPhone AR placement requires a physical-device check and is not simulated by desktop WebKit.
