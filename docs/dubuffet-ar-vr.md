# Dubuffet AR/VR release

La Chiffonnière starts at an explicitly approximate 6.7 m overall height, anchored to Friends of Embarcadero Plaza’s published 22 feet. The exact sculpture-versus-base extent remains unverified. The complete upright model and round base are retained; no stretching or new plinth is applied automatically. The reference is bound to the current model URL and orientation.

Source: https://embarcaderoplaza.com/tour/la-chiffonniere

The release also includes the previously tested physical-reference infrastructure and independent display supports. Later research and 32 new calibrations remain in the separate working copy.
